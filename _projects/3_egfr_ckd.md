Predictive modeling of **chronic kidney disease (CKD)** progression from longitudinal estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) data, classifying patients by their probability of stage degeneration to support virtual care management. Presented as co-authored work at SIAM Mathematical Problems in Industry.

**Tools:** Python (pandas, scikit-learn, Matplotlib).
