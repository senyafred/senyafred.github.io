How does oxygen availability drive **reductive stress** in a *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* biofilm? This project couples genome-scale metabolic modeling (flux balance analysis) with reaction–diffusion PDEs to capture how metabolism plays out across space and time. A key contribution is measuring reductive stress as a **flux** — the NADH a cell is forced to dump without oxygen — and showing that the biofilm core naturally becomes oxygen-limited and high-stress.

This is the basis of my first-author preprint (*Senya et al.*, bioRxiv 2026).

**Tools:** Python, COBRApy, SBML (iSD1509 genome-scale model), reaction–diffusion PDEs.
