Teaching is central to who I am. As an **Instructor of Mathematics at the University of Vermont**, I have taught 45–51 students per section across five courses spanning **Algebra, Pre-Calculus, Calculus, and Modern Mathematics**. I use student-performance data to adapt my instruction, and I work hard to build classrooms that are inclusive and respectful — something my student evaluations consistently reflect (4.4–4.8/5 for inclusive climate).

Earlier, I lectured on **Computer & Digital Literacy** at Cape Coast Technical University in Ghana, and served as a **Computational Mathematics Teaching Assistant** at the University of Ghana, supporting instruction, grading, and mentoring. I have also mentored K–12 scholars as an educator with Envision by WorldStrides.

### Courses taught

- Modern Mathematics
- Calculus
- Pre-Calculus
- College Algebra
- Computer & Digital Literacy (Cape Coast Technical University)
